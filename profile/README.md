# AtomTech

**AI, Big Data and Automation Solutions**

Technology consultancy in Lanzarote for businesses. Automate processes, make
decisions with your data and grow with enterprise AI.

- **[DRAGO](https://drago.atomtech.es)** — The go-to digital companion for
  anyone in Lanzarote: residents, tourists and professionals. Second prize at
  the Impulsa Startup Demo Day, and more than 800 users in its first month.
- **[LICITA-PRO](https://licita-pro.es)** — Continuously tracks Spanish public
  tenders (PLACSP) and grants (BDNS), and analyses tender documents with AI.
- **[MAGEC](https://magec.atomtech.es)** — Automates hospital shift scheduling,
  respecting contracts and rest periods.

Working with the Cámara de Comercio de Lanzarote y La Graciosa, MiHUB and the
Confederación Empresarial de Lanzarote.
